## Bat facts that transfer (cited)
- Pulse rate tracks TIME-TO-CONTACT, not distance; terminal buzz ends a FIXED
  TIME (47-63 ms = reaction time) before contact (Melcón 2007). Search-phase
  rate is locomotor-locked (one call/wingbeat).
- Beam WIDENS 40°→90° entering the buzz (Jakobsen & Surlykke 2010) — narrow
  far for SNR, wide near because angular rate explodes.
- **Bats deliberately refuse to encode range as loudness** (AGC: −11 dB
  hearing per halved range, Kick & Simmons 1984). Our constant-amplitude
  rate ticker = biologically correct; keep.
- Strobe groups = attention re-clustering at CONSTANT call budget (Kothari
  2018) → scan-coaching analogue.
- Jamming: bats go SILENT near conspecifics (76% within 1 m) or call
  louder — not retune. Irrelevant to our ToF pair (repo already resolved:
  raised noise floor, not phantoms; geometry edge-to-edge; time-multiplexed).

## Human echolocation — LOCKED CONSTRAINTS
- Protected band **2–5 kHz** (clicks peak 3.4 kHz; echoes −27 dB below the
  click; users defend +12 dB SNR at 4–5 kHz — experts AND novices alike).
- **FFT of our actual tones: tick (600 Hz) −75 dB in-band, earcon (1250 Hz)
  −70 dB — safe by accident. A "crisper" 3 kHz beep would be 0 dB = dead
  centre. NEVER brighten the tick.** (Now documented in CALLOUT-PROTOCOL.)
- Never occlude either ear (blind ILD sensitivity is ELEVATED for echoes);
  avoid sustained/noise-like textures (deny binaural masking release);
  sparse transients fine.
- Echolocation acuity is better 45° OFF-axis than dead ahead (Thaler 2022)
  → device confidence should weight toward CENTRE (our center motor covers
  the user's own weakest zone).
- White space (hedged): no published device yields to / triggers on the
  user's own clicks.
- "FlashSonar" = branding, not a peer-reviewed construct; cite the papers.

## The two code bugs found

### BUG 1 — ticker saturates + is speed-perverse
Current: rate caps at 6.67 Hz from 800 mm inward — the most urgent 0.4 s
carries no information; and a walk from 2.5 m yields 5 ticks at 1.0 m/s but
only 1 tick at 1.8 m/s. FIX (implemented 2026-08-20): TTC-based —
rate = clamp(5/TTC, 0.8, 12); silent above TTC 2 s (standoff heartbeat
inside 0.5 m); **TTC ≤ 0.6 s → categorically different TERMINAL sound** (not
a faster tick — humans fuse trains ~20-30 Hz). Properties: ~6 ticks per
approach INDEPENDENT of speed; terminal fires at a fixed time margin like
the bat's; a stationary user goes silent (kills the BuzzClip failure mode).
Frame rate is NOT the bottleneck for TTC (σ≈12 ms at 1.2 m); association is.

### BUG 2 — fixed 15° path cone is narrower than a torso up close
±15° at 0.3 m = ±8 cm. A pole you're about to clip with a shoulder is
outside it; we use 2 of 8 columns at ALL ranges. FIX (implemented):
half_angle = min(atan2(0.35 m, range), 45°) → 11° far, 41° at 0.4 m — beam
widening derived from body clearance, independently confirmed by the bat
literature.
