# Commercial blind-wearables fate survey — 2026-08-18 (sub-agent, condensed verbatim)

Every dedicated blind/low-vision wearable of the last decade, priced and
status-checked (2026-08-18). **Zero of eight reached ADOPTED.**

| Device | Price | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Envision Glasses | $1,899–3,499 | ABANDONED — Google Glass supply died; all editions sold out |
| Ally Solos (Envision pivot) | $399–699 + sub | NICHE — "felt cheaper than Meta glasses", "processing time is eternal" |
| eSight Go | ~$4,950 | NICHE — survives on VA reimbursement, not consumers |
| WeWALK Smart Cane 2 | $850+$5/mo | NICHE — experienced users return it within weeks (false-positive alerts) |
| Sunu Band | ~$299 | DEAD — company closed, domain for sale |
| biped NOA | €1,850–2,750 | NICHE — **75 units shipped in 2024**; best-reviewed detector, killed by price+bulk |
| .lumen | €9,999 | NICHE — Romania-only, 3–6 month lead |
| Aira Horizon | $89+/mo | ABANDONED 2020 — died when Samsung killed its host phone |
| OrCam MyEye | $3,689–4,490 | DEAD — glasses division closed Jul 2024; ~90% valuation wipeout; company's own filing blames "generative AI on smartphones" |

## The two recurring killers
1. **Dedicated hardware dies with its supply chain** (Aira→Samsung phone,
   Envision→Google Glass).
2. **A $4k single-purpose device cannot survive free multimodal AI on a
   phone** — OrCam's own words.

The only commercial escape found: institutional reimbursement (eSight/VA).
The category's own pivot (Envision): abandon dedicated hardware, ride
consumer glasses, sell subscription — and even that ships "quirky."

## What this means for the helmet
- As a PRODUCT category this is a graveyard — which is fine: ours is a
  research prototype and portfolio piece, and honesty about that IS the
  credible position (matches the claims-language rules).
- The de facto winning platform is the phone + consumer glasses. Our unique
  defensible slice stays: continuous head-height depth sensing + haptics —
  the thing phone AI structurally can't do (no depth, in a pocket).
- biped NOA at 75 units despite being "the best package" = even excellent
  obstacle detection doesn't sell at €2k. Utility features (what the
  daily-life stream ranks) carry adoption, not detection alone.
- WeWALK lesson repeated: false positives are worse than nothing —
  experienced users RETURN devices over them. Our silence-default +
  hysteresis design is the counter.
